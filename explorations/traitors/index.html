The Premise

Welcome to the Castle

Thirty seven top residents of Atria arrive at an ancient castle (A1008) to play for one shared prize pot. Hidden among them are the Traitors, chosen in secret and known only to each other. Everyone else is an Innocent.

The Innocents must root out the Traitors before the 
Traitors murder them, one by one.

The Roles

Innocents and Traitors

The Innocents

The majority. They don’t know who the Traitors are.

  • Win missions to get Shield or Powers
  • Debate and vote at the Circle of Shaque to banish suspects
  • Win by banishing every Traitor before the end

The Traitors

A secret few, chosen by the host on the first night.

  • Hide in plain sight among the Innocents
  • Meet each night to murder one Innocent
  • Win by surviving to the end and stealing the pot

How to Play

A Day in the Castle

I

City Sleeps

Everyone closes their eyes.

II

The Kill

Traitors open their eyes and decide a unanimous Kill

Everyone opens their eyes and the Kill is communicated, the Kill continues to play the game (w/o voting power)

III

Circle of Shaque

Accusations, defences and a vote. One player is banished and God decides whether they reveal their identity.

⟳

The process repeats

During the game there will be some rounds of tasks/missions where everyone gets a chance to earn a shield or a power.

The Game

By Evening

The Circle of Shaque

01

Accuse

Players voice suspicions and defend themselves against the room.

02

Vote

Each player writes one name in secret, then reveals it in turn.

03

Banish

Most voted person leaves the game for good. 
A tie goes to a revote.

04

Reveal

Whether the banished person reveals his/her identity is upto the God.

The Game

By Night

Murder in the Turret

When the Innocents go to bed, the hooded Traitors slip away to the Turret and agree on one Innocent to murder.

The victim receives a sealed letter, leaves the castle quietly, and simply never appears at breakfast.

Traitors cannot murder each other

A shield blocks one murder

The Game

A Twist in the Dark

Recruitment

When the Traitors lose one of their own, the host may let them replace their numbers by turning an Innocent.

The Offer

Join us, or walk away

A chosen Innocent is summoned in secret and offered a hood. They may accept and become a Traitor, or decline and stay Innocent.

The Ultimatum

Join us, or die

When a lone Traitor remains, the offer can become a threat: refuse the hood and be murdered that same night.

The Game

Protection

Shields

Earned

Won during missions, sometimes by a single player, sometimes by gamble.

Secret

The holder decides whether to reveal it. A hidden shield keeps the Traitors guessing.

One night only

It stops a murder, never a banishment at the Circle of Shaque.

The Game

The Endgame

The Final Fire

The last players gather around the fire. Each one secretly votes on a single question: is the game over?

End the game

If every vote says end, the game stops. Roles are revealed and the Winner is decided.

Banish again

If anyone suspects a Traitor remains, the group must banish once more, then vote again.

The Game

House Rules

Laws of the Castle

I

Keep your secret

No one may prove their role. No "I swear on my dog". 
Everyone may claim to be Innocent.

II

No virtual connections

Phones are surrendered. No messages in or out.

III

Votes are public

Every Circle of Shaque vote is shown to the room and explained.

IV

Banished means gone

A banished or murdered player is out of the game and doesn't talk after that.

V

Night is sacred

Everyone keeps their eyes closed when it's the "City Sleep" state. Any misconduct leads to banishment by God.

VI

host's word is final

Every twist, ruling and reveal comes from the host alone.
