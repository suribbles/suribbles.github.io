About Me

Every career story needs a plot twist. Mine happened at IIT Bombay.

I arrived there as an engineer, as any good small-town kid was expected to. Back then, Engineering and Medicine were the only two "real" careers, and my love for sketching was always considered as a hobby. Then I found out the campus had a Design school. Plot twist accepted. I switched lanes and have happily stayed there since.

That was 2008. Since then I've designed at HP, Microsoft, ADP and ServiceNow, moved between Mumbai and Hyderabad, and filled more sketchbooks than I can count.

Outside of work, I sketch, teach, and talk to anyone who will listen about why investing in design at the right time, pays for itself. Home is its own creative studio: my wife is a chef, and our 10-year-old son and 5-year-old daughter make sure no idea goes unquestioned.

These days AI has me as excited as a kid with a new box of crayons. I'm looking for roles where I can bridge design and technology, using the latest AI tools to build things that are smart and feel human.

The small-town kid with the sketchbook is still here. He just has better tools now.
