Brand

One body. One earth. One collective.

A branded house: the master name leads, and each sub-brand adds one word and one signature colour. Everything should feel like a slow morning outdoors: unhurried, honest, warm and quietly energising.

Typography

A warm serif, a quiet sans

Fraunces carries the personality and Inter carries the details. Use sentence case, tight tracking on big headlines, and one italic word for emphasis.

Voice & imagery

Calm, warm, direct. Never preachy.

Say it like this

  • Short sentences and gentle invitations: “Come move with us.”
  • Sensory words: breathe, blend, ground, stretch.
  • Camel-case names, full lockup first: OneCollective GoodCrowd.
  • 3–5 specific hashtags, alt text on every post.

Show it like this

  • Early-morning or golden-hour daylight. Ochre is this light.
  • Real bodies of all ages, Indian settings: terraces, courtyards, parks, beaches.
  • Linen, cotton, cane, clay, whole seasonal fruit, wet leaves.
  • Leave a third of the frame quiet for text.

Claims guardrails

  • No “eco-friendly” on its own. Say what it is: “100% organic cotton, GOTS certified.”
  • Smoothies never cure, treat or “detox”. Nutrition claims need lab values; check FSSAI rules.
  • No hype, body-shaming or fear-based health copy.
